Backstory

I. The Sorrowind Choir

The Sorrowinds are an old elven family with an odd trade: they sing for the dead. Nobles across the Sword Coast hire them to perform laments at funerals. Their tradition says a lament frees the soul like a bird into the sky. A soul nobody sings for stays stuck. Nyx is the youngest of them, barely of age by elven reckoning and about twenty to look at. Her older sister Lark taught her every song she knows.

II. The Story She Tells

Ask Nyx why she left home and you'll get a great story. She got bored at a dull lord's funeral and made up a verse about how he'd "finally found something more lifeless than his dinner parties." The mourners laughed, the widow screamed, and the family threw her out. She tells it in every tavern in Baldur's Gate, and it gets a laugh every time. It isn't true.

III. The Truth

Lark died the winter Nyx turned twenty. Nobody in the family will say exactly how, and Nyx doesn't believe it was an accident. Nyx was chosen to sing Lark's lament. She stood at the graveside, opened her mouth, and nothing came out. Then she ran. Nobody threw her out. She left, and she hasn't been able to sing a lament since.

IV. The Mask

The three black birds on her cheek are for Lark. She inked them herself so her sister's soul would have a way out, since Nyx couldn't give her one. The black clothes are mourning she never took off. The loud, cocky tavern songs are there so nobody notices she never sings a sad one. The mind flayers grabbed her mid-show in Baldur's Gate. Her first feeling wasn't fear. It was outrage that they'd interrupted her.

"Everyone dies, darling. The trick is making sure someone sings for them."Nyx, not looking anyone in the eye
Background: Entertainer. She's been performing for crowds since she could stand.
Her arc: somewhere in all this, she finds the voice to sing Lark's lament. Save it for a big moment, like a companion's death, a funeral in Act 3 or the end of the game.
Open thread: what actually happened to Lark? Leave it vague so you can tie it to anything the story throws at you.

How to play her

Cocky on the outside, grieving underneath. Play her loud most of the time and let the mask slip when death is in the room. That contrast is what makes her fun to play.

Personality

  • TraitTheatrical and vain. She treats every room as a stage and every conversation as a performance with an audience.
  • TraitMorbidly cheerful. She jokes about death constantly, usually at the worst possible moments.
  • Mask slipAround graves, grief and the dying, the act stops. She goes quiet and gentle, then cracks a joke a moment later as if nothing happened.
  • IdealNobody goes unsung. Everyone deserves someone to remember them, even the villains.
  • BondLark's violin. It was her sister's, and it's the only thing she took when she ran.
  • FlawShe won't sing a lament. Ask her to and she'll change the subject with a joke. She also can't let an insult go unanswered.

Dialogue compass

  • Pick the clever line over the safe one. If there's a Deception, Performance or Persuasion check, Nyx tries it.
  • Punch up, never down. Mock tyrants, bores and bullies freely. Spare the weak, the scared and the strange.
  • Respect the dead. She'll loot a crypt, but she says a few words over the bones on the way out. Desecrating a body is a line she won't cross.
  • Let the mask slip sometimes. When a companion opens up about loss, choose the kind, honest reply.
  • Keep the drama. When a choice gives you a bigger story to tell later, take it.

Subclass

3LEVEL
You pick your college at your very next level-up. It isn't permanent. Withers can respec Nyx at camp for 100 gold, so if a college doesn't feel right after a few fights, you can switch.

College of Swords · recommended

The duelist. You already dual-wield a shortsword and dagger, and Swords turns that into a real fighting style. Flourishes spend Bardic Inspiration to add damage and effects to your attacks. It's flashy and it puts her centre stage, which suits a cocky show-off.

Weapon damage
Spellcasting
Toughness
Fits Nyx

You get

  • Medium armour and scimitar proficiency
  • Fighting Style: take Two-Weapon Fighting for your current setup
  • Flourishes: Slashing (hits two enemies), Defensive (+4 AC on a hit), Mobile (knocks back, then you teleport to them). Each has a ranged version for crossbows.
  • Extra Attack at level 6

Watch out for

  • Flourishes eat Bardic Inspiration, so you'll have less to hand out
  • She's in melee range, so she'll take hits. Fight next to Hagra, not out ahead.

College of Lore

The pure caster. Pick this if you find you like Faerie Fire and Vicious Mockery more than swinging your sword. It's often rated the strongest bard college, but it plays from the back line.

  • Cutting Words: a reaction that spends Inspiration to make an enemy's attack miss
  • Two spells from any class's list at level 6, well before other bards

Watch out for

  • Your shortsword and dagger become mostly decoration
  • Squishy. Keep her behind Hagra.

College of Valour

The battle bard. It's sturdier than Swords, with shields and martial weapons, but less flashy. With Hagra already holding the front line, you probably don't need the extra toughness.

The enchanter, added in Patch 8. It's all about charm and temporary hit points. The flavour is great for a diva, but most tier lists rank it the weakest bard college.

Spells worth having

  • Healing Word: Nyx has it too, so you have two ways to get downed allies up.

Build notes

Key picks

  • Level 3, Pact of the Blade: the natural Hexblade choice, and it unlocks Thirsting Blade.
  • Level 5, Thirsting Blade invocation: her Extra Attack. Don't miss it.
  • Agonizing Blast: adds Charisma to Eldritch Blast for when she needs to fight at range.
  • Feats: pump Charisma first, since it powers both her weapon hits and her spells.

Playing with Nyx

  • Short rests matter. Warlocks get their spell slots back on a short rest, and Nyx's Song of Rest gives the party an extra one.
  • Hold Person, then Curse: guaranteed crits on the paralysed enemy, plus extra damage from Savage Attacks.
  • Hagra holds the line with the Everburn Blade while Nyx flourishes around her.
  • Charisma gear: they'll both want the same items, so agree who gets what.
